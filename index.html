realmz.io — Free Multiplayer Browser Battle Game

realmz.io is a free multiplayer io game you can play right in your browser, with no download or install required. Fight other players and powerful enemies, level up your character, unlock new weapons and skins, and dominate the realm in fast-paced PvP battles.

How to play realmz.io

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, attack to defeat enemies and other players, collect stars and loot, and climb the leaderboard. Create a free account to save your progress, earn coins, complete missions, and customize your character with skins.

Features

  • Free to play multiplayer io game in your browser
  • Real-time PvP battles against players around the world
  • Level up, unlock weapons, and upgrade your character
  • Collect coins, complete daily missions, and unlock skins
  • Global leaderboards, clans, and an active community

Join the realmz.io community on Discord, and learn more about the game or read the latest updates.
